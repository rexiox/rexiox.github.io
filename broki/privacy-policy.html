Privacy Policy for Wurfi

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

At Wurfi ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Wurfi mobile and smartwatch application (iOS & Apple Watch).

1. Information We Collect

Wurfi functions as a lightweight web browser companion for smartwatches and smartphones. To deliver core browser features, we process the following data:

  • Bookmarks and History: Bookmarks managed on your iPhone and web search/browsing history performed on your Apple Watch are stored locally on your devices and synced via secure system channels (e.g., iCloud/Watch Connectivity). We do not collect, view, or store your browsing history on external servers.
  • Search Queries: Search inputs entered on the Apple Watch are sent directly to your chosen default search engine to display query results.
  • Device and Usage Data: Anonymous technical data—such as device model, OS version, app performance, and crash reports—may be collected to optimize application speed, stability, and UI layout on wearable screens.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the processed information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide core browser functionality, including web page rendering, search execution, and history navigation on Apple Watch.
  • To sync bookmarks smoothly between your iPhone and connected smartwatch.
  • To maintain app performance, stability, user experience, and security across devices.
  • To respond to user support requests and customer service inquiries.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Wurfi does not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal data or browsing history to third parties. Data is shared only under the following strictly limited conditions:

  • Search Engines and Web Content Providers: When you execute a search or visit a web page, queries and standard browser headers (such as User-Agent) are sent to the respective third-party search engine or web host to retrieve results.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party platforms (e.g., Apple App Store infrastructure) that facilitate app distribution, operating systems sync, and anonymous error reporting.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or official legal request to protect our rights or comply with regulatory obligations.

4. Data Security & Local Storage

Your browsing history and bookmarks are designed to remain private. Wurfi leverages iOS and watchOS system-level security, including sandboxing and TLS/SSL encryption protocols for web traffic, to keep your data safe. Sensitive browsing data is kept locally on your hardware unless synced through your personal encrypted cloud services.

5. Children's Privacy

Wurfi does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to remove such data.

6. Your Rights and Data Control

You maintain full control over your data. You can clear your search history and remove bookmarks directly within the Wurfi app settings on your iPhone or Apple Watch at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
