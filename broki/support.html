Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I sync bookmarks to my Apple Watch?

Add or edit your bookmarks inside the Wurfi iPhone app. They will automatically sync to your connected Apple Watch via Watch Connectivity.

2. How do I clear my search and browsing history?

You can clear your browsing history at any time directly within the Settings menu inside the Wurfi app on your Apple Watch or iPhone.

3. Why is a web page not displaying correctly on Apple Watch?

Apple Watch has limited screen real estate and browser capabilities. Wurfi optimizes web content for small screens, but some heavy desktop sites may not fully render.

4. Does Wurfi store my personal browsing data?

No. Your search history and bookmarks stay stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud storage. We do not track or store your browsing data on external servers.
