How it works

STEP 1

Get the iPhone app

Download Macropad from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

STEP 2

Install the Mac Helper

Download Macropad Helper on your Mac and move it to Applications.

STEP 3

Allow Accessibility

Enable the Helper in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

STEP 4

Pair and press

Scan the QR code shown by the Helper with your iPhone. That's it.
