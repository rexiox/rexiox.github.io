02What we collect

We do not collect personal data through the apps. We don't operate servers that receive your boards, actions, keystrokes, window titles, files or usage history. We can't see what you build or what you trigger.

The only information that reaches us is:

  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include (for example a description of the problem, screenshots, or an exported board). We use it only to answer you.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics — only if you allow it. If you've enabled “Share with App Developers” in your iPhone or Mac settings, Apple may share anonymized crash logs and performance data with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
  • Aggregate App Store statistics. Apple provides developers with aggregated, non-identifying figures such as download counts and sales. These never identify you.

04iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Macropad can sync your boards between your devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. That data is stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to it — we can't view, export or restore it on your behalf.

You can turn sync off in Macropad's settings or in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. You can delete synced data from Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

05Local network & pairing

When you scan the QR code shown by Macropad Helper, your iPhone and Mac exchange a pairing key and connect directly over your local network. Each time you press a key or turn a knob, the iPhone sends a small command (for example “send ⌘B” or “volume +2”) to your Mac, which performs it.

  • These commands are not routed through our servers or any third party.
  • The QR code contains only what's needed to find and authenticate your Mac (such as a local address and a one-time pairing secret). It's processed on your device.
  • Your Mac does not send its screen, files or typed text back to the iPhone. It may send small status updates — for example connection state or the current volume level — so on-screen controls can reflect it.

07Purchases

Macropad Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, card details or billing address. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that Pro is active on your Apple Account, so it can unlock Pro boards and packs. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payment data.

08Third parties

Macropad contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and we don't sell, rent or share personal data. The only external services involved are Apple's own: the App Store, In-App Purchase, iCloud/CloudKit and, if you opt in, crash reporting.

Actions you assign may control other apps on your Mac (for example Xcode, Zoom or Music). Macropad only sends them the command you configured; how those apps handle your data is governed by their own policies.

09Retention & deletion

  • On your devices: deleting a board removes it; deleting the app removes its local data. Unpairing a Mac deletes its pairing key on both sides.
  • In iCloud: remove it via iCloud storage settings as described above.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable follow-up period (normally no longer than 24 months), then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK and California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Authority — Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

Because we hold almost no data about you, in practice this means your support correspondence. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. Where we process support messages, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, or your request itself.

11Children

Macropad is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.
