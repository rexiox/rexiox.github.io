Macropad Helper for Mac

The small companion app that runs on your Mac and carries out what you press on your iPhone. Free, lightweight, and it lives in your menu bar.

Install in a minute

STEP 1

Download

Click the button above to download MacropadHelper.dmg.

STEP 2

Move to Applications

Open the disk image and drag Macropad Helper into your Applications folder.

STEP 3

Allow Accessibility

On first launch, turn on Macropad Helper in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

STEP 4

Pair your iPhone

Open Macropad on your iPhone and scan the QR code shown by the Helper. Done.

If macOS won't open it

Macropad Helper is distributed directly rather than through the Mac App Store. If you see a message that it “can't be opened”:

  1. Try to open Macropad Helper once, then dismiss the message.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Macropad Helper, then confirm.

You only need to do this once. Always download the Helper from this page or our official GitHub releases.

Questions

Why isn't the Helper on the Mac App Store?

To press keyboard shortcuts and manage windows for you, the Helper needs system-level Accessibility access. That kind of control isn't possible within the Mac App Store's sandbox, so we distribute the Helper directly. The iPhone app is on the App Store as usual.

Is it free?

Yes. Macropad Helper is free. Pro features are unlocked in the iPhone app through In-App Purchase.

Does the Helper send my data anywhere?

No. It talks only to your paired iPhone over your local network and doesn't log your typing. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I update it?

Download the latest version from this page and replace the old app in your Applications folder. Your pairing and settings are kept.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Macropad Helper from the menu bar, then move it from Applications to the Trash. You can also remove it from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

My iPhone can't find my Mac.

Make sure both devices are on the same network, the Helper is running, and Local Network access is allowed for Macropad on your iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). More help on the Support page.
