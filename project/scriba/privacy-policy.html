Scriba Privacy Policy

Scriba is a simple writing app. Everything you write stays on your device — not on our servers.

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Applies to Scriba for Android and iOS

00At a glance

No accountYou never sign up or log in to use Scriba.
No trackingNo ads, no analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
Stays on your deviceYour notes, folders, images and voice notes are stored only on your phone.
No serversWe don't run servers that receive your writing.

01Who we are

Scriba is developed and published by Rexiox (“we”, “us”). For the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and similar laws, we are the controller of the limited personal data described here — mainly the messages you choose to send us.

02What we collect

We do not collect personal data through the app. Your notes, folders, images, voice notes and settings are never sent to us, and we can't see what you write.

The only information that can reach us is:

  • Support messages. If you email us at contact.rexiox@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to answer you.
  • Crash reports — only if you allow it. Google Play and Apple may share anonymized crash and performance data with developers when you have enabled this in your device settings.
  • Purchases. Scriba Pro is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. We never see your payment details.
  • Aggregate store statistics. Google Play and the App Store give developers aggregated, non-identifying figures such as install counts.

03Data stored on your device

DataPurpose
Notes, folders and their contentsShowing and editing your writing
Images, drawings and voice notes you add to notesShowing and playing them inside your notes
Earlier versions of each noteVersion history, so you can look back at or restore an earlier version. Up to 50 versions per note are kept, and older ones are removed automatically.
Settings: theme, font, language, passcode lock, editor preferences, writing goals, read-aloud speed and voiceRemembering your preferences. The passcode is stored encrypted with your device's secure key store.

This data stays inside the app's private storage. It leaves your device only through your own system backup (see below), or when you choose to share or export a note or save a backup file.

04Device backups

Android: if Android backup is on, your notes, images and voice notes can be included in your device backup to your own Google account. Encrypted settings, including your passcode, are excluded from backups.

iOS: notes, images and voice notes are part of your iCloud or computer backup if you have device backup enabled.

These backups are handled by Google or Apple under your account. We have no access to them.

Backup files: Back Up Data in Settings saves all your notes, folders and attached media to a file in a place you choose. The file is not encrypted and is never sent to us; keep it somewhere safe.

05Permissions

  • Photos: when you add an image, Scriba opens the system photo picker. Only the image you pick is shared with the app; Scriba does not request access to your photo library.
  • Microphone (and Speech Recognition on iOS): used only for dictation and voice notes, and only when you start one in the editor. Before the system asks for this permission, Scriba explains why it needs it, and you can decline and keep using the rest of the app. Your speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS); Scriba prefers on-device recognition when it is available, otherwise that service may process the audio online under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. For dictation, Scriba does not record or store your voice — only the resulting text is added to your note. Voice notes are recordings you choose to make: they are saved inside the note on your device, are never sent to us, and you can remove them at any time. You can turn this permission off at any time in your device settings.
  • Files: importing and exporting notes (.txt, .md, .docx, .pdf, .html, .scriba) and saving or restoring backups use the system file picker. Scriba only reads or writes the file you choose.
  • Spell check: uses your device's built-in spell checker. Your text is not sent to us.
  • Read aloud: uses the text-to-speech voices on your device and needs no permission. The text is read on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Internet: used to load the support and legal pages shown inside the app, to check your Scriba Pro status, and for app updates and ratings through Google Play or the App Store. Your notes are never uploaded.
  • Sharing: when you share a note, the system share sheet sends it to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

06Third parties

Scriba contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data.

  • RevenueCat manages Scriba Pro purchases as described above (RevenueCat privacy policy).
  • Google and Apple provide the app stores, payments, in-app updates and rating prompts and, for dictation, the system speech recognition service (Google privacy policy, Apple privacy policy).

07Retention & deletion

You can delete any note or folder inside the app. Uninstalling Scriba removes all of its data from your device. To remove copies in your device backups, manage them in your Google or Apple account settings. Support emails are deleted once your question is resolved, unless we need them for legal reasons.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK), you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Since we only hold support emails, write to contact.rexiox@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

09Children

Scriba is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
